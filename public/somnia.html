A NOTE ABOUT SOMNIA

A gentle companion, not a sleep tracker.

Dream Builder uses generated speech to repeat your intention while the page is open. It doesn't detect REM sleep, monitor sleep stages, or run reliably with your device locked. Choose a wake time and keep the page open while you wind down. AI summaries and the generated voice need a valid API key. On macOS, Somnia falls back to the installed Mac voices when the key is unavailable.

Community intentions and votes are shared across visitors. Do not post private information. Use the flag button to report an intention that should be reviewed. Speech uses voices available on your device unless hosted AI speech is configured. This site does not detect sleep stages or keep your device awake.
