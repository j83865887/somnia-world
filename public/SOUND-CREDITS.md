# Somnia sound library

Somnia bundles short excerpts from openly licensed recordings. These are included locally, so presets work without an audio service.

- `bubble-pops.wav`: 16 edited bubble-wrap transients by Anthousai, CC0 1.0. Source and editing notes: [bubble-wrap-simulator audio credits](https://github.com/finktheartist/bubble-wrap-simulator/blob/main/public/audio/CREDITS.md).
- `mechanical-keyboard.mp3`: “Keyboard Typing 6 (HHKB, Topre)” by grcekh, Freesound CC0 1.0. Source credit and re-hosting statement: [stay-for-dogs test audio sources](https://github.com/vighriday/stay-for-dogs/blob/main/public/test-audio/SOURCES.md).
- `rain-window.mp3`: “rain - outside window.wav” by Anthousai, Freesound CC0 1.0. Source credit and re-hosting statement: [stay-for-dogs test audio sources](https://github.com/vighriday/stay-for-dogs/blob/main/public/test-audio/SOURCES.md).

The bubble recording is a 16-hit atlas; the app seeks to one hit and stops playback before the next. The keyboard typing recording is used for randomized short key-click excerpts. The window rain recording loops quietly when that preset is selected.
